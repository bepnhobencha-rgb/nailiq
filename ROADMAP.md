# Roadmap

Phased delivery for NailIQ. Durations and milestones are TBD; order reflects dependency and risk reduction.

| Phase | Name | Focus (summary) |
|--------|------|-----------------|
| **0** | Foundation | Repo, standards, design tokens, memory/docs, environment, and baseline quality. |
| **1** | Landing + Register | Public surface and account creation. |
| **2** | Setup | Tenant/salon configuration, services, and basics to go live. |
| **3** | Booking Engine | Availability, calendar, and client booking flows. |
| **4** | Payment | In-person and/or online payment paths as scoped. |
| **5** | AI + Automation | AI modules and workflow automation. |
| **6** | Analytics + Admin | Reporting, admin tools, and operational visibility. |
